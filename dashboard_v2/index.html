Dashboard Interpretation Guide

How the AI-Engine Works

Key Metrics Explained

  • Shortage Risk (0-100%): The mathematical probability of critical food delivery shortfalls occurring within 3 months, calculated using weather metrics, news sentiment, and trade friction.
  • Food Price Index (FPI): The projected market value of consumer agricultural goods, where 100 serves as the normalized benchmark baseline.

The Two Forecasting Backends

  • RF Baseline (Random Forest): An ensemble of Decision Trees trained on-the-fly directly inside your browser runtime. Very fast and lightweight.
  • PyTorch GPU (Neural Network): A Multi-Layer Perceptron (MLP) trained locally on your system's RTX GPU using advanced neural layers. Deeply traces complex cross-market dynamics.

3-Month Lead Forecasting

  • Causal Lags: The model reads historical indicators at month $t$ (precipitation, oil prices, CPI, etc.) to predict target indexes at month $t+3$.
  • 2026 Forecast: Shows predictions for January, February, and March 2026 computed from actual indicators recorded at the end of 2025.
